Welcome, Daughter of the King

At Mfangano Central SDA Women's Ministry, we believe every woman is a masterpiece created by God with a unique purpose. Our ministry is a sanctuary where women of all ages come together to grow in faith, find encouragement, and serve our community with love.

Through Bible study, prayer, fellowship, and outreach, we equip women to be lights in their homes, churches, and the world.

"Her children arise and call her blessed; her husband also, and he praises her: 'Many women do noble things, but you surpass them all.'"
— Proverbs 31:28-29
