🎼 About Our Music Ministry

Our Music Ministry is the heartbeat of worship at Mfangano Central SDA Church. We believe that music is a powerful tool for evangelism, edification, and connecting with God.

Through various choirs, instrumental groups, and vocal ensembles, we strive to offer our best to God and inspire our congregation through Spirit-filled music.

"Let the message of Christ dwell among you richly as you teach and admonish one another with all wisdom through psalms, hymns, and songs from the Spirit, singing to God with gratitude in your hearts."
— Colossians 3:16
