Our Vision & Mission

Building Christ-like character in every young adventurer

👁️

Our Vision

To raise a generation of young people who love God, serve humanity, and shine as lights in their communities — equipped with spiritual, physical, and mental strength for life's journey.

🎯

Our Mission

To lead boys and girls (ages 10–15) to Jesus through Christ-centered activities that build character, instill discipline, develop skills, and inspire a lifelong commitment to service and evangelism.

💎

Our Values

Faith in God • Loyalty & Obedience • Purity & Health • Service to Others • Love for Nature • Teamwork & Discipline • Joyful Worship

Our Pledge & Law

The foundation of every Pathfinder's journey

⚜️ The Pathfinder Pledge

By the grace of God,

I will be pure, and kind, and true;

I will keep the Pathfinder Law;

I will be a servant of God and a friend to all.

⚜️ The Pathfinder Law

1. The morning watch is the Pathfinder's duty.

2. The Pathfinder is loyal and true to God and to his leaders.

3. The Pathfinder keeps the alert.

4. The Pathfinder is a cheerful friend.

5. The Pathfinder is courteous and obedient.

6. The Pathfinder walks softly in God's great outdoors.

7. The Pathfinder does a good turn every day.

8. The Pathfinder keeps himself physically strong, mentally awake, and morally straight.
