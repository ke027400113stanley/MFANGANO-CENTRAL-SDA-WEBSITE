⬇

Welcome to God's Family

Mfangano Central Seventh-day Adventist Church is a thriving community of believers located on Mfangano Island in Homa Bay County, Kenya. For decades, we have been a beacon of hope, faith, and service to our island community and beyond.

Our church is more than a building — it is a family united by our love for Jesus Christ, committed to worship, fellowship, discipleship, and ministry. We welcome everyone, regardless of background, to experience the transforming love of God.

"For where two or three gather in my name, there am I with them."
— Matthew 18:20

Whether you're a lifelong member or a first-time visitor, you'll find a warm, welcoming home here at Mfangano Central SDA.

Our Journey Through the Years

A legacy of faith, growth, and God's faithfulness

1950s

The Beginning

A small group of believers began meeting for worship on Mfangano Island, laying the foundation for what would become a thriving church.

1970s

Church Established

Mfangano Central SDA Church was officially organized, with the first church building constructed through the dedication of early members.

1990s

Growth & Expansion

The church experienced significant growth, launching new ministries including Pathfinders, Youth, Women's, and Men's ministries.

2010s

Modern Era

Renovation of church facilities, establishment of health ministry, and expansion of community outreach programs across the island.

2026

Today & Beyond

Continuing to grow in faith, embracing technology, and reaching the community with the three angels' messages through various ministries.
