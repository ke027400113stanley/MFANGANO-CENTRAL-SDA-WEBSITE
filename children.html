📖 Today's Bible Story

Noah's Big Boat! 🚢

God told Noah to build a huge boat because a big flood was coming. Noah obeyed God even when others laughed. God sent animals two by two into the ark. Then it rained for 40 days and 40 nights! But Noah and his family were safe because they trusted God. After the flood, God put a beautiful rainbow in the sky as a promise! 🌈

Remember: God keeps His promises!
