Featured Ministry

Community Outreach & Health Ministries

Our Community Outreach and Health Ministries team is the hands and feet of Jesus on Mfangano Island. We organize free medical camps, health education seminars, and community feeding programs that bring hope and healing to the most vulnerable.

Through partnerships with local clinics and global health organizations, we provide essential services and share the love of Christ in practical ways.

"Go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them... and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you." — Matthew 28:19-20

Join us every third Sabbath for our community service day. All are welcome to serve!
