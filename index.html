Our Vision

Rooted in Faith, Reaching Out in Love

Mfangano Central SDA Church is more than a building — it's a family of believers transformed by the grace of Jesus. Located on the beautiful Mfangano Island in Lake Victoria, Kenya, we have been a beacon of hope for nearly a century.

Whether you're a long-time member or visiting for the first time, you'll find a warm, authentic community here. We worship together, grow in faith, and serve our island community with the love of Christ.

"Come as you are — you are loved, you belong, and you matter to God and to us."
