Welcome to Our Church Family

Rooted in Faith, Reaching Out in Love

Mfangano Central SDA Church is more than a building — it's a family of believers who have been transformed by the love of Jesus. Located on the beautiful Mfangano Island in Lake Victoria, Kenya, we have been a beacon of hope for nearly a century.

Whether you're a long-time member or visiting for the first time, you'll find a warm welcome here. We worship together, grow in faith, and serve our community with the love of Christ.

"Come as you are — you are loved, you belong, and you matter to God and to us."

Join us as we journey together in faith, discovering the depth of God's grace and sharing it with the world around us.
