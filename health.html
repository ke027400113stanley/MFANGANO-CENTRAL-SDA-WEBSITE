Our Health Services

Comprehensive healthcare services offered by our Health Ministry

Free Health Screening

Regular blood pressure, diabetes, and general health checkups every Sabbath after church service.

🙏

Prayer for Healing

Dedicated prayer teams available for anointing and prayer for the sick. Contact us anytime.

📚

Health Education

Monthly health seminars on nutrition, disease prevention, mental health, and healthy living.

💚

Counseling Services

Professional Christian counseling for mental health, addiction, family issues, and grief support.

🏠

Home Visits

Our health team visits bedridden members and the elderly at home for prayer and health checks.

🏥

Hospital Referrals

We help connect you with trusted hospitals and specialists in the Mfangano area and beyond.

Simple steps to access our health ministry services
