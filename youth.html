Our Mission & Vision

To empower young people to live victorious Christian lives, develop their God-given talents, and impact their generation for Christ. We believe that the youth are not just the church of tomorrow, but the church of today.

Through dynamic worship, intentional discipleship, and community outreach, we are building a generation that is bold in faith and compassionate in action.

  • Spiritual Growth & Discipleship
  • Leadership Development
  • Community Outreach & Evangelism
"Let no one despise your youth, but be an example to the believers in word, in conduct, in love, in spirit, in faith, in purity."
1 Timothy 4:12 (NKJV)

Our Core Activities

We offer a variety of programs designed to nurture your faith, build friendships, and develop your unique talents.

Youth Sabbath

Every 2nd Sabbath of the month. Experience dynamic worship, powerful testimonies, and relevant messages tailored for young adults.

AY Society

Friday evenings at 5:00 PM. A vibrant time of fellowship, biblical debates, talent showcases, and spiritual enrichment.

Sports & Recreation

Weekly sports activities including football, volleyball, and athletics. Building teamwork, discipline, and physical fitness.

Youth Outreach

Quarterly evangelism campaigns and community service projects. Reaching our peers and neighborhoods with the love of Christ.

Music & Drama

Join our worship teams, choir, or drama skits. Expressing faith creatively and leading the congregation in spirited praise.

Mentorship Program

One-on-one guidance from experienced church leaders. Support for career choices, spiritual growth, and personal development.
